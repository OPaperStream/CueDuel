# Privacy Policy

Cue Duel does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission and never connects to a server.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Settings, statistics and replays are saved only on your device and are deleted
  when you uninstall the app.
- The only permission the app uses is vibration, for haptic feedback.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/OPaperStream/CueDuel/issues
